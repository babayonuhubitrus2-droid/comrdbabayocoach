Cybersecurity Threat Awareness

A practical cybersecurity awareness project focused on understanding common cyber threats and developing safer digital habits.

Cybersecurity Threat Awareness Online Safety Defensive Security

Project Objective

The objective of this project is to study common cybersecurity threats and explain practical steps that individuals can take to reduce their exposure to attacks.

Threats Covered

  • Phishing and suspicious messages
  • Malware and malicious downloads
  • Weak and reused passwords
  • Account takeover risks
  • Unsafe websites and links
  • Social engineering awareness

Security Practices

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Verify suspicious links before opening them.
  • Keep operating systems and applications updated.
  • Avoid downloading files from untrusted sources.
  • Be careful when sharing personal information online.

What I Learned

This project helped me understand how common cyber threats affect everyday users and why basic security practices are important for protecting accounts, devices, and information.

Project Status

This is a learning and portfolio project. It can be expanded with additional research, screenshots, security demonstrations, and documented findings as the project develops.
